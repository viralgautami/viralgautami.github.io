Gate-Pass Stress Lab

Entry gates fail in two ways: the network drops, and people try to get in twice. This lab issues cryptographically signed QR passes, validates them offline, queues admissions in IndexedDB, and then lets you break it — cut the network, replay a pass at two gates, or flood the line with thousands of scans.

01 Issue passes

An ECDSA P-256 key signs each pass. Gates hold only the public key — a stolen scanner can verify passes but can never mint one.
